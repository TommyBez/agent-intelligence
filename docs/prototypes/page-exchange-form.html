In what form does an agent read and write a page?

One page, one agent (Scout) and one human (Marta). Pick a form, press the buttons, and watch three things after every press: what Scout sends, what Scout receives, and what the page looks like afterwards. The three forms are kept pure so their differences show; they could be combined.

Everything here is simulated in this file. No MCP client was connected and no agent was run. Sizes are characters of the call or result written as JSON. The description of a task is the same kind of thing as the body of a page, so whatever holds here holds there.
